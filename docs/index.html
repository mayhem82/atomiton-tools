Test reports, limitations & adoption instructions

The research need

The supplied records had no mission-specific comparison view. Null, empty and absent fields needed to remain visible.

Who it is for

People already comparing records while navigating a system, and researchers working alongside them.

Take it on

  1. Download and unzip the complete tool bundle.
  2. Open comparison.html for the synthetic working example.
  3. Read GUIDE.txt and inspect the test reports and source.
  4. For your own records, run: python compiler.py your-records.json --out your-comparison.html
  5. Keep the original records and report trial outcomes with the exact tool version.

Limits to carry forward

  • No real-person field trial has been performed.
  • The tool organises what supplied records say; it does not establish eligibility, current availability or a remedy.
  • Missing information remains unknown. The comparison gap is partly addressed under the declared technical checks.
  • 2–500 flat records; 2–30 fields; strings or null; up to 512 KB.
  • Creating a new dataset view requires Python 3.11+. The supplied example opens directly in a browser.

Keep the evidence with the tool.

Every entry has conditions. Make them visible.

01 / Access

A usable free path.

The listed function must work without payment. Accounts, limits and dependencies are disclosed. Paywalled functions do not qualify for the library.

02 / Provenance

Credit where it came from.

Existing tools keep their original source and licence. Evidence of use by others is recorded alongside what the author reports and what Atomiton actually checked.

03 / Trial

A result with its limits.

Synthetic checks, practical trials and observed benefit have separate records. Failed and mixed results stay useful. A passing test carries its version and conditions.
