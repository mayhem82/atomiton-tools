# Discoveries, tests and trials

The catalogue covers tools found in investigations, including existing tools already used by others, and instruments built or adapted in response to evidenced gaps. The target is practical benefit for people already dealing with the researched system.

Publication needs an identifiable function, original source, version, licence, usable free path and enough instructions for someone else to take it on. A paywalled function does not qualify. A free account can be disclosed as a requirement only when the listed function remains usable without payment. Record any operational or quota limits. Optional paid functionality is outside the entry's listed and tested capability.

For an existing tool, cite the original provider/repository and licence. Keep evidence that others use it separate from marketing claims, popularity counts and our own tests. Do not mirror third-party binaries or source without the applicable redistribution rights.

Every test or trial record identifies who conducted it, the version, date, conditions, procedure, actual outcome and retained evidence. Author-reported tests are labelled as such. Atomiton's own checks are labelled as such. A field trial describes actual use in an identified setting with its limitations; a synthetic fixture is not a field trial. Failed, mixed and unresolved results remain useful evidence and should be retained alongside successes.

An entry can be available as a prototype before a field trial. It must say so. A tool built solely for research is eligible on the same terms. Creating a tool does not close the research gap; record what its application changed and what remains unresolved.

The catalogue does not recognise new canonical structural harm mechanisms. Such candidates belong in their research register, derived from source-bound Lattice Atlas type work and pending the user's recognition.

To propose a change, edit catalogue.json and the tool's source/evidence, run scripts/build.py and scripts/check.py, then open a pull request. The public page and package hashes are generated from those records. Keep private case records and credentials outside contributions; publicly shareable synthetic or appropriately scoped evidence is sufficient for technical checks.
